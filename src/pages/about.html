Meet Selin

Hello, my name is Selin, and I have been a hypnotherapist since 2018. I specialise in addressing low self-worth and its various manifestations, such as recurring life patterns, procrastination, addictions, narcissistic abuse trauma and more.

Who am I

After earning a degree in Computer Science Engineering, I worked as a software engineer. I first came across hypnosis in 2013 at a self-development festival, where I attended a group hypnosis session. The hypnotist invited us to work on a memory that bothered us the most. My choice was immediate: a violent physical attack I had experienced at age 15 that left me feeling unlovable, isolated, and haunted for years afterward. That haunting stayed with me until that day.

After the session, the memory no longer affected me. I healed from it. Now, when I look back, the memory is still there, dim and distant, but, more importantly, neutral. How could one group hypnosis session, not even a private one tailored to me, have such a profound impact?

Fast forward to 2015. I went through a very traumatic breakup with my then-boyfriend, who was also my tango partner. Around that time, I met an incredible NLP facilitator (you can think of hypnotherapy as an umbrella term that includes NLP). I began working with her to process my emotions and understand how my low self-worth had led me into that relationship in the first place. I attended sessions religiously for over a year, almost weekly, to heal.

During that time, I learned about narcissistic abuse and, to my surprise, not only did my negative emotions lift, but I also came out of depression. Hypnosis gave me a healthy outlet to express my emotions, and I truly thrived during that year of deep inner work. How was I able to overcome such a major trauma through hypnosis alone?

After experiencing the profound effects of hypnosis firsthand, I decided to explore the field more deeply and began studying hypnotherapy in 2016. Through my studies and case work with clients, I discovered my passion for facilitating sessions and witnessing transformation in others. My analytical skills turned out to be a major advantage as a facilitator, and client feedback affirmed my natural ability, my ikigai, which only deepened my love for the mind and for hypnotherapy.

Since then, I’ve been committed to continuous learning and have been seeing clients since 2018. I’m deeply passionate about the mind, constantly expanding my knowledge, staying current with new research, and, let’s face it, I’m a bit of a nerd about it all. Most recently, I completed the Grace Method Hypnotherapy School in 2024.

Through hypnotherapy, I’ve healed from childhood trauma, overcome my phobia of driving, built unshakable confidence, improved my public speaking, quit my sugar addiction, and attracted new opportunities into my life.

I’m also a daily meditator, beach lover, and yoga teacher/founder of Bloom Yoga Ballina. I walk my talk, I test new tools on myself, receive weekly hypnotherapy sessions, and practice self-hypnosis daily.
